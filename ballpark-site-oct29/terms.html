Terms

Last updated October 29, 2026

Estimates are not quotes

Every price a Ballpark Leads calculator shows is an estimate based on the answers given and typical jobs. It is not a bid, quote or contract. The final price is set in writing by the HVAC company, usually after an in-home visit, and can change with what the technician finds.

Homeowners

Ballpark Leads does not charge homeowners anything. Any work, maintenance plan or payment is between you and the HVAC company.

HVAC companies: prices

You set the prices your calculator uses, through your price-book form. You are responsible for those prices and for the quotes and work you provide to your customers.

HVAC companies: billing

Each calculator is billed month to month at the price shown on our pricing page. You can cancel any time, and your calculator stops at the end of the month you cancel in. The setup fee is paid once, when your calculator goes live on your site. The free demo costs nothing.

Maintenance plans

When a homeowner signs up for your maintenance plan through your calculator, we send you the sign-up. You bill the homeowner directly. Ballpark Leads does not collect plan payments.
