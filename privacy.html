Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Who we are

Ballpark Leads builds estimate calculators that HVAC companies put on their own websites. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in one of those calculators. Questions? Email ballparkleads@gmail.com.

What the calculators collect

When you use a calculator, it may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your home address or ZIP code
  • Details about your heating and cooling system, such as its type, size, age, model number or a photo of its label
  • The option you picked and the price range you were shown
  • For maintenance bookings, the visit time you chose

Who gets it

Your information goes to the HVAC company whose website the calculator is on. That company may call, text or email you about your estimate or booking. We also keep a copy so the calculator can work, send you your estimate and fix problems.

We don’t sell it

We don’t sell the information you enter in a calculator.

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete your information, email ballparkleads@gmail.com with the email address or phone number you used. We’ll confirm once it’s done. To have the HVAC company delete its copy, contact that company directly.

HVAC companies who sign up with us

If you request a demo on this site, we use what you send us (your name, company, contact details, website and service area) to build your demo and follow up with you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
