Pricing FAQ

What does “try it free” include?

We build a working demo of your calculator with your prices and send you a link to try it. No card needed. You pay the $250 setup only when you decide to put it live on your site.

How does pay-per-lead work?

You load a $25 prepaid lead credit. Each lead costs $2–$5 depending on your trade, and the credit reloads automatically so you never miss one.

Is there a contract?

No. There's no signed agreement — short terms are accepted at checkout, and you can stop anytime.

Will it work with my website?

Yes. It embeds on WordPress and most website builders, and on the landing pages behind your Google ads.

Who gets the leads?

You do. Every lead goes to your inbox first, then can forward automatically to your CRM or team.

Are the prices accurate?

They're your prices. The calculator gives homeowners a ballpark range based on how you price jobs, and you confirm the final quote.
